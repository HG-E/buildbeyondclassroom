# Partnerships and Sustainability (Gate 8)

Oct 15, 2026 · Ekene

## What this gate decides

BBC works with six kinds of partner, admits each through the same four stages, and funds itself in a fixed order: grants first, institution licences once the pilot has produced evidence. Learners stay free or low-cost, as Gate 1 set out.

- **Partner types:** hub hosts, regional partners, community partners, employers and mentors, funders and an independent evaluator.
- **Partnership path:** conversation, pilot agreement, hub certification, renewal, with a gate between each.
- **Agreement terms:** seven clauses every partner signs, taken from Gates 2, 3, 6 and 7.
- **Funding order:** the five streams from Gate 1, tested against BBC's principles and sequenced by stage.
- **Link to Gate 7:** the first institution that pays BBC is the trigger to move off Vercel's free plan.

## Partner types

Each partner type gives BBC one thing it cannot supply alone, and receives something specific back.

| Partner | What they give | What they get | What BBC requires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hub host (school, university room, library, NGO centre, community hall) | A safe space and shared devices | Certified hub status, trained facilitator, the toolkit | A signed data protocol and a named data owner |
| Regional partner | Hosting and supervision of several hubs | Standards, facilitator training, support from the core team | Hub checks, incident reporting, no running of sessions without a certified facilitator |
| Community partner (NGO, local government, company with a real problem) | A real community problem and access to it | Learner work on their problem; solutions documented openly | A named contact who confirms the need and the outcome |
| Employer or field mentor | Time, field knowledge, review of solutions | A view of emerging talent | Mentor conduct rules; no recruitment of learners during a cohort |
| Funder (foundation, corporate foundation, development agency) | Money for cohorts, facilitators or hubs | Reports on youth skills and community outcomes | No control over learner data, content or evaluation findings |
| Independent evaluator | An evaluation design and findings | Access to anonymised programme data | Design agreed before the pilot starts, as Gate 1 advised |

Facilitators are not a partner type. They are certified individuals who work through hubs, as Gate 6 set out.

## How a partnership grows

Every partner moves through four stages in the same order, and no cohort runs until Gate B is passed.

Gate A checks the partner accepts the seven clauses. Gate B is the Gate 6 rule: a certified facilitator, a signed data protocol and a named data owner. Gate C asks for a safely finished cohort with results shared before renewal.

## What every partner agreement must say

Every agreement carries the same seven clauses, so the programme can open a hub in a new country without redesigning anything. This is a content checklist for counsel to turn into legal text, not legal wording.

1. **Safeguarding.** Under-13s take part only in facilitator-led sessions; minors only in facilitator-run sessions. The facilitator signs the Do-No-Harm Check (Gate 3).
2. **Data.** A signed data protocol and a named data owner (Gate 6). Minimal data, no tracking, no profiling, no sale or sharing with advertisers (Gate 7).
3. **Open content and brand.** Programme content and documented solutions stay open. Partners may use the BBC name only as the agreement allows, and may not present their own programme as BBC.
4. **Learner ownership.** The learner owns their portfolio and can export it. A partner may use a learner's solution only with that learner's written consent.
5. **AI use.** The Gate 3 use modes, unaided checks and stop rules apply at every site, whatever tool is used.
6. **Standards and reporting.** A certified facilitator at every cohort; hub checklist met; anonymised results shared with the core team and the independent evaluator.
7. **Independence and exit.** Payment buys no say over content, learner data or evaluation findings. Either side can leave; learners in a running cohort finish it, and the partner returns or deletes data as agreed.

## Funding and revenue options

Of the five streams in Gate 1, four fit BBC's principles if the safeguard in the table is written into the agreement; the fifth should stay off until there is evidence.

| Stream | Fit with principles | Main risk and safeguard | Effect on hosting (Gate 7) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Philanthropic and development funding | Strong: pays for access to be free | A single funder steering content. Safeguard: clause 7; no funder above a set share of income | Grant money that pays for site work may count as commercial on Vercel's wording |
| Studio licence for schools and universities | Good if it charges for training and support, not for access to open content | Closing the content. Safeguard: content stays open under clause 3 | Counts as commercial; move host |
| Community and employer challenges | Good: real problems, real outcomes | Learners used as cheap labour. Safeguard: learners own their solutions (clause 4); no recruitment during a cohort | Counts as commercial; move host |
| Facilitator certification | Good: builds local ownership | Excluding people who cannot pay. Safeguard: sponsored places funded from grants | Counts as commercial; move host |
| Optional learner-side services | Weak: untested, and it risks the free-or-low-cost promise | Charging the learners BBC exists to reach. Safeguard: keep out of the core until the pilot shows demand | Counts as commercial; move host |

The hosting column is the practical point: the first paying institution or paid site work is the moment to leave Vercel's free plan for Cloudflare Pages or Netlify, as set out in Gate 7.

## Sustainability

BBC is built to be cheap to run: the platform is almost free, so the real costs are people and places, and each has a named payer. No budget figures are set here; the pilot budget is built from real quotes (see open items).

| Cost | Pilot stage | At scale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facilitator time and certification | Grants, with sponsored places | Hubs and regional partners; certification fees with sponsored places |
| Hub space, shared devices, connectivity | Hub host, largely in kind | Hub host and regional partner |
| Core team: standards, training, office hours, safeguarding channel | Grants | Licences and grants |
| Independent evaluation | Grant, ring-fenced | Grants; shared across partners |
| Platform: domain, hosting, database | A domain of about $10 to $15 a year (approximate); hosting and database free | Hosting reviewed once income starts (Gate 7) |
| Legal: legal form, agreements, data protection advice | Grant or pro bono counsel | Licences |

**Funding changes by stage**

1. **Pilots (three contexts):** grants and hub hosts' in-kind support. Nothing is charged to learners.
2. **After the first pilot:** studio licences and facilitator certification begin, using the pilot's evidence.
3. **At scale:** community and employer challenges, and regional partners that fund their own hubs.

**The sustainability test.** BBC passes if it can lose any one funder and keep running, and if a hub can keep running cohorts from the open playbook without the core team in the room.

## Risks and safeguards

The biggest risks come from money and growth, not technology; each has a safeguard already in this gate or an earlier one.

| Risk | Safeguard | Where set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One funder dominates and steers content | Income cap per funder; clause 7 on independence | Funding options, clause 7 |
| Licences quietly close the content | Charge for training and support, never for access to content | Clause 3 |
| A partner misuses the BBC name | Brand use limited by the agreement; right to withdraw hub status | Clause 3 |
| A safeguarding incident at a partner hub | Certified facilitator, Do-No-Harm Check, incident reporting to the core team | Clauses 1 and 6 |
| A partner asks for learner data | Data protocol and named owner; no sharing beyond the agreement | Clause 2 |
| Quality drops as hubs multiply | Hub checklist, sampled build logs, recertification of facilitators | Gate 6, clause 6 |
| A partner leaves mid-cohort | Learners finish the running cohort; data returned or deleted | Clause 7 |
| Free hosting terms are breached when income starts | Move to Cloudflare Pages or Netlify before the first invoice | Gate 7 |

## Decisions to confirm and open items

Five decisions need a yes before partner conversations start, and five items are still open.

**Decisions (recommended: yes to all)**

- [ ] The six partner types and what each gives and gets.
- [ ] The four-stage partnership path, with a gate before each stage.
- [ ] The seven agreement clauses as BBC's standard terms, with counsel drafting the legal text.
- [ ] Nothing charged to learners during the three pilots; grants and in-kind hub support first, licences and certification after the first pilot's evidence.
- [ ] Sponsored facilitator places funded from grants, so certification fees never exclude people.

**Open items**

- A cap on how much of BBC's income any one funder may provide. The share is not set.
- Legal form of BBC, which decides who signs agreements and receives grants (carried from Gate 1).
- The first pilot's hub host and community partner, by name.
- An independent evaluator to agree the evaluation design before the pilot starts.
- A pilot budget built from real quotes for facilitators, hub costs, evaluation and legal advice.
