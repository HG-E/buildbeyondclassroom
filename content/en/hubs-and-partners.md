# Hubs and partners

BBC is delivered through facilitated cohorts. The rule for choosing how a site runs is simple: use the most human contact the site's resources allow, and add virtual sessions only to reach what a hub cannot.

## Ways to run a cohort

- **Physical hub.** The cohort meets in person each week at a hub, with virtual sessions for experts and reviews.
- **Hybrid.** In-person meetings plus a reliable connection and a room set up for virtual sessions.
- **Virtual, live-facilitated.** For learners who cannot reach a hub, with small teams, a facilitator and a local community partner for each team.

A hub can be a university room, a school classroom, a library, an NGO centre or a community hall.

## What a hub needs before it opens

A hub runs a cohort only when it has both of these:

1. A certified facilitator.
2. A signed data protocol with a named data owner.

Hubs aim to provide shared devices, so that no learner needs to own a device to take part.

## Who BBC works with

| Partner | What they give | What they get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hub host | A safe space and shared devices | Certified hub status, a certified facilitator and the toolkit |
| Regional partner | Hosting and supervision of several hubs | Standards, facilitator training and support |
| Community partner | A real community problem and access to it | Learner work on their problem, documented openly |
| Employer or field mentor | Time, field knowledge and review | A view of emerging talent |
| Funder | Money for cohorts, facilitators or hubs | Reports on learner skills and community outcomes |
| Independent evaluator | An evaluation design and findings | Access to anonymised programme data |

## What every partner agrees to

Every agreement carries the same seven commitments:

1. **Safeguarding** of young people.
2. **Data** protection, with a named data owner.
3. **Open content**, and use of the BBC name only as the agreement allows.
4. **Learner ownership** of their own work.
5. **AI use** within the programme's rules.
6. **Standards and reporting**, including a certified facilitator at every cohort.
7. **Independence and exit.** Payment buys no say over content, learner data or evaluation findings.

Every partner moves through four stages in the same order: a conversation, a pilot agreement, hub certification and renewal. Hub certification needs a certified facilitator, a signed data protocol and a named data owner, and no cohort runs until it is passed. Renewal needs a safely finished cohort, with results shared.
