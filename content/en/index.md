# Build with AI. Solve a real problem. Own the result.

Build Beyond Classroom (BBC) helps people learn to use AI to build solutions to problems in their own communities, and to make the business case for those solutions in their own field of study or work. It is not specific to one country, and it goes beyond the classroom.

> BBC is being designed and has not launched. The first pilot has not been announced. This site will be updated as decisions are made.

## What every learner does

1. **Builds with AI.** Learners use AI tools to design, build and publish something real.
2. **Finds and checks a community problem.** The need is confirmed with the people who live with it, not assumed.
3. **Makes the business case in their own field.** Who benefits, who pays, what it costs, what rules apply and who else already serves the need.
4. **Owns the result.** The work belongs to the learner and can be taken with them.

## What makes it different

- **The measure is a shipped project.** Success is a solution that real people use, confirmed by a community partner, not attendance.
- **A person guides every group.** Learning happens in teams with a facilitator, at a hub, in a hybrid format or in live online sessions. BBC does not offer a self-paced online course as its core.
- **AI helps you learn; it does not do the thinking for you.** Short checks with the tool closed show whether the learner has learned or only produced.
- **Light and open.** Programme content is open and works on weak connections. Information about learners is protected.
