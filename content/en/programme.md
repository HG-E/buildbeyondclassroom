# The programme

BBC is built around one idea: people learn to use AI best by building something real that helps their community. The programme has three journeys, three levels and a ladder of skills.

## Three journeys

- **Explore.** A first look at how a field works as a business and which path might suit you. It is facilitator-led and is offered only after local advisers have cleared the age and consent rules for the country.
- **Build.** The core journey. A team works through a real community problem from need to launch, with a facilitator.
- **Launch.** Taking an existing solution into a second community, with a path to funding or income.

## Three levels

1. **Understand and use.** Use AI tools well and safely.
2. **Apply and build.** Use them to build solutions.
3. **Create and scale.** Take a solution further, with a verified portfolio and a path to funding or work.

The levels follow [UNESCO's AI competency framework for students](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-students) (Understand, Apply, Create) and the [OECD and European Commission AI literacy framework](https://digital-skills-jobs.europa.eu/en/latest/news/new-ai-literacy-framework-common-reference-integrating-ai-literacy-education), so schools and funders can see where BBC fits.

## A ladder of skills

Learners climb seven rungs. Tools are only supporting actors; the rungs describe what a learner can do.

1. Chat and research with AI
2. Create content and design
3. Use no-code and AI app builders
4. Automate tasks and build assistants
5. Write code with AI
6. Deploy and publish
7. Operate and measure what you have built

## Your own field

Every learner looks at their own subject through five questions:

1. Who benefits?
2. Who pays?
3. What does it cost?
4. What rules apply?
5. Who else already serves this need?

## Who can take part

BBC is for learners of different ages and backgrounds. Learners under 13 take part only in facilitator-led sessions. The first pilot is a facilitated eight-week Build journey for adults aged 18 and over. Facilitators are also trained and certified, as explained on the [facilitators page](/facilitators/).

BBC's plan is to keep access free or low cost for learners, with institutions and funders paying for cohorts. This is still being tested, and no prices have been set.
