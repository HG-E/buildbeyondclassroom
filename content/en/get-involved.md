# Tell us who you are and what you want to do.

BBC has not opened applications. You can register interest now, and we will contact you when there is something to join. For now, the way to do that is email.

## Prepare your message

Choose who you are, add a few words, and open the message in your email app.

{{interestTool}}

## Or write to us directly

{{emailBox}}

Please do not include sensitive personal information, or any information about children. Read how BBC handles information in [safeguarding and data](/safeguarding-and-data/).

## Who can write to us

{features 3}
- @learner | Learners | To hear when a cohort opens near you. The first pilot is for adults aged 18 and over; younger learners are not yet in scope.
- @users | Facilitators | Educators and community leaders who would like to be trained and certified.
- @hub | Hub hosts | Schools, universities, libraries, NGOs and community halls that could host a cohort.
- @search | Community partners | Organisations with a real community problem that learners could work on.
- @briefcase | Mentors and employers | People who can give time and field knowledge.
- @funder | Funders and evaluators | Organisations that want to support or independently assess the programme.

## What to put in your email

{checks}
- Who you are and which of the groups above you belong to.
- The country or region you are in.
- One or two sentences on why you are interested.
